Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the excludedculture Pinterest app ("the app") handles data. The app is operated by the owner of the excludedculture Etsy shop.

1. What the app is

The app is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by the shop owner to manage the shop's own Pinterest business account: creating and scheduling Pins for the shop's own product listings, organizing them into boards, and viewing analytics for those Pins. The app is not offered to the public or to any third party.

2. Data the app accesses

Through the Pinterest API, the app accesses only data belonging to the owner's own Pinterest account:

  • Account information (username, account type, profile details)
  • Pins and boards created by the account
  • Analytics for the account's own Pins and boards

The app does not access, collect, or store personal data of any other Pinterest users.

3. How the data is used

The data is used solely to publish and organize the shop's own content and to understand how that content performs. It is not used for advertising profiles, tracking individuals, or any purpose unrelated to managing the account.

4. Sharing

Data obtained through the Pinterest API is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

5. Storage and security

API access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own device, are never published, and are never shared. Data retrieved from Pinterest is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

6. Deletion and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account's security settings, after which the app can no longer reach the account. Any stored data is deleted on request or when the app is no longer in use.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision.
